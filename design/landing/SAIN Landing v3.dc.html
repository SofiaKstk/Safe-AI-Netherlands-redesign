Safe AI Netherlands

Preparing the Netherlands for advanced AI

Artificial intelligence is improving faster than the institutions meant to govern it. SAIN builds the Dutch community of researchers, engineers and policy thinkers working to keep these systems under human control — through free courses, a national research hub and career support.

The mission

SAIN is preparing the Netherlands for AGI

The Netherlands has the universities, the engineering talent and the policy weight to matter in the transition to advanced AI. The people working on it are scattered across faculties, ministries and companies that rarely meet. We connect them, and we train the next group.
