01 — The mission

SAIN is preparing the Netherlands for AGI.

Frontier AI systems are improving faster than the institutions meant to govern them. The Netherlands has the universities, the engineering talent and the policy weight to matter in that transition — but the people working on it are scattered.

We connect them. SAIN runs free technical courses, a national research hub, and career support for people moving into AI safety full time.
