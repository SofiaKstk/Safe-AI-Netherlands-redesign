Call for applications
What it is

A supervised research programme for Dutch students and professionals working on interpretability, evaluations and governance. Twelve weeks, part time, mentored by researchers from Dutch universities and frontier labs.

Cohorts run twice a year from Utrecht, with two on-site weekends and weekly supervision online. Output is a paper, a replication or a policy memo — reviewed by the mentor and published in our archive.
